Our Story

A Pharmacy Built on Trust

Maa Bhawani Drug Store has been serving the people of Dighwara and surrounding areas since 1989. What started as a humble local pharmacy has grown into a trusted healthcare institution — a name that families in Ami Mor, Dighwara, and nearby villages rely on for genuine medicines, honest advice, and compassionate service.

Owned and operated with deep personal commitment, our store ensures that every medicine dispensed is authentic, every prescription is handled with care, and every customer is treated like family. We understand that healthcare is not just a business — it's a responsibility.

100% Genuine & Verified Medicines
Every product sourced from licensed distributors. No counterfeit, no compromise.
Experienced & Knowledgeable Staff
Decades of hands-on experience in medicines, dosages, and patient guidance.
Home Delivery for Your Convenience
