Maa Bhawani Drug Store has been serving the people of Dighwara and surrounding areas since 1989. What started as a humble local pharmacy has grown into a trusted healthcare institution — a name that families in Ami Mor, Dighwara, and nearby villages rely on.

Every medicine is authentic, every prescription handled with care, and every customer treated like family.
